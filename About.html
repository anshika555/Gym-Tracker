ABOUT FITTRACK

Your Fitness. Your Progress. Your Journey.

WHO WE ARE

FitTrack is a simple and user-friendly fitness tracking website designed to help people manage their fitness journey in one place.

Users can record their workouts, set fitness goals, monitor their progress and keep track of their nutrition.

Whether you are a beginner or an experienced gym enthusiast, FitTrack helps you stay organized and consistent with your fitness routine.

WHAT WE OFFER

Workout Tracking

Record exercises, sets, repetitions and weights to keep track of your workouts.

Fitness Goals

Set goals such as weight loss, muscle gain, strength or general fitness.

Progress Tracking

Monitor your weight, BMI, workout streaks and overall fitness progress.

Diet Planning

Keep track of your meals, calories, protein and water intake.

OUR GOAL

Our goal is to make fitness tracking simple, organized and motivating.

We want to help users stay consistent, understand their progress and become a stronger version of themselves.
